# Guven Bircan

**Industrial Operations & Engineering | Lean/TPS | Cost Improvement**

I am a mechanical engineer based in Belgium. My professional background includes more than 19 years at Toyota Motor Europe across manufacturing engineering, R&D and cost improvement.

At Toyota, I served as the primary engineering-side interface with Purchasing for cross-functional cost-reduction work, including planning and proposing budgets and allocating approved funding to engineering divisions. Annual programme savings increased from approximately €5M to over €20M through a cross-functional programme involving Engineering, Purchasing and suppliers, a collective team result. I was recognized through three Toyota Value Analysis (VA) Awards, including individual and team awards.

I am registered as self-employed in Belgium and currently work as an independent contractor through Mercor, reviewing AI-generated engineering and manufacturing material. I also explore **AI-assisted software development** through personal learning projects.
