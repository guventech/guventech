# Guven Bircan

**Operational Excellence & Cost / Value Engineering Leader | Mechanical engineer | Lean/TPS**

I am a mechanical engineer based in Belgium. My professional background includes more than 19 years at Toyota Motor Europe across manufacturing engineering, R&D and cost improvement.

At Toyota, I served as the primary engineering-side interface with Purchasing for cross-functional cost-reduction work, including planning and proposing budgets and allocating approved funding to engineering divisions. Annual programme savings grew from about €5M to over €20M a year by 2024, a team result. My own part: I built a dashboard that pulled cost-reduction ideas from the tracking app and showed them as a process flow, with the value and the responsible department for each idea. Using 80/20, we focused on the few high-value ideas that drove most of the savings. I was recognized through three Toyota Value Analysis (VA) Awards, including individual and team awards. Participated in joint engineering, purchasing and supplier cost-improvement activities during the Corolla launch, including supplier workshops and on-site plant work. Worked on the wireless phone charger supplier change for the next-generation Corolla, saving €4M+.

I am registered as self-employed in Belgium and currently work as an AI Training Specialist, independent contractor through Mercor, since July 2026, reviewing AI-generated engineering and manufacturing material. I also explore **AI-assisted software development** through personal learning projects. Results from my roles at Toyota Motor Europe were employment, not client engagements. I have not taken consulting clients.
